pispot display mockups

Each page is a fixed 1280×720 stage, the size of the 5" Touch Display 2 in landscape, scaled to fit your window. Fake data; press 1–5 on any page to change the power scenario. Add ?kiosk to hide the switcher.

DSKY

Apollo Guidance Computer homage. Type VERB 16 NOUN 30 ENTR for power, N40 for network, V05 N40 for the client list, V35 for a lamp test.

Plain

Power rows with icons and colour-coded voltages, weather, and a network summary. Tap the network card for the map.

Network map

Hosts with pictures and notes; dotted lines move while traffic flows.

LCARS

Star Trek TNG console look.
